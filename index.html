C1V1 = C2V2 Dilution Formula

Conservation of mass in liquid solution transfer. Ensures compound stock diluted into media matches target concentration ($C_2$) without solvent toxicity ($V_1$).

V1 = (C2 × V2) / C1
N/P Charge Ratio Equation

Determines the molar ratio of protonated amine groups (N) in transfection polymers/lipids to anionic phosphate groups (P) in nucleic acid backbones.

Moles N = (Mass polymer) / (Mw per N unit)
4PL Hill Equation (IC50)

Models sigmoidal drug inhibition. $Y = \text{Bottom} + \frac{\text{Top} - \text{Bottom}}{1 + 10^{(\log IC_{50} - X) \cdot \text{HillSlope}}}$.

Viability % = 100 / (1 + (Conc / IC50)^H)
